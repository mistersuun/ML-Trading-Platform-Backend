# P2 Leader First-Pullback Budget Call Spread, from candidate M1 (momentum lens)

Frozen before the backtest ran.

FROZEN SPEC v1 (2026-11-14). Paper research only, no orders.
HARNESS: identical to P1. SPY calendar; off-calendar bars dropped; IS entries 2021-11-05..2025-10-31, HO 2025-11-03..2026-11-13. RV20 as P1. Black-Scholes APPROXIMATION with r=4%, q=0, IV_u = clip(1.25 x RV20_u, 0.15, 2.50) re-marked nightly. Synthetic Friday/third-Friday expiries. Strike grid <$25 $0.50, $25-100 $1, $100-250 $2.50, >=$250 $5. Half-spread per leg max($0.05, 3% of mid) each side, plus $0.65 per contract per leg per side. Fills at the model price on the decision close. $1,000 at 0%. Governor: halve target risk below 0.85 x HWM; halt new entries below $600. Seed 20261114.
UNIVERSE: all symbols, ETFs included, except SGOV, IEF, SEQ, SEQUX, CBRS, GOOG, FFH and PSHD. A symbol needs >= 252 valid bars up to t.
SIGNAL (close of t), all must hold: R126 = Close_t/Close_{t-126} - 1; RSpct = its cross-sectional percentile rank (pandas rank pct=True) among eligible symbols with valid R126 on t. (1) RSpct >= 0.90; (2) Close_t > SMA50_t; (3) on at least one of sessions t-10..t-1, Close equalled the 63-session rolling max of Close ending that session; (4) Close_t < min(Close_{t-5..t-1}). Lockout: ignore a signal on a symbol within 10 sessions of its previous signal, whether or not that one was traded. At most 1 new entry per night: the highest RSpct, ties broken by larger R126. If that one is skipped as over budget, the next-ranked one is tried. Live-only filter, not in the backtest: skip if earnings fall within the next 10 trading days.
INSTRUMENT: bull call vertical, 1 lot, because a 1.05 x Close long call fits the $80 cap on only about 3% of signals. Expiry = the first monthly with DTE >= 25. K1 = nearest strike to Close_t. K2 = nearest strike to 1.10 x Close_t, or K1+inc if that is <= K1. Cost = 100 x (ask K1 - bid K2) + commissions. If cost > min(0.08 x equity, $80), lower K2 by one increment at a time, down to K1+inc. If it still does not fit, SKIP and log. Diagnostic only, not traded: the per-trade return of the unconstrained 1-lot 1.05 x Close long call with the same expiry and exits, which makes the result comparable with the probe.
SIZING: 1 lot per bet. Max loss = cost <= min(8% equity, $80) (the governor halves this to 4%). Max 3 concurrent positions. No adds and no averaging.
EXIT (each close u > t): (a) take profit if mark >= min(4.0 x entry debit, 0.80 x width); (b) time exit at the close of the 10th trading day after t. No price stop. The earnings exit is live-only.
STRESS RUNS: A: IV mult 1.5 at entry, 1.25 after. B: 1.5 everywhere. C: 1.0 everywhere (report only). D: half-spreads doubled. E: one-session delay of entry and exit.
CONTROLS: (b, primary) MATCHED. For each calendar year, draw the same number of random (symbol, day) pairs satisfying rules 1-2 (RSpct >= 0.90 and Close > SMA50) but not rule 4, with an identical instrument, budget rule, exits and 1-lot sizing. 1,000 draw sets; report the strategy's percentile on mean per-bet return and on the $1,000-engine terminal equity (same 1-per-night and 3-concurrent rules). (a) Random 3% of all valid symbol-days, same structure. (c) SPY buy-and-hold $1,000 price-only, from 2021-11-05 and reset on 2025-10-31.
METRICS (IS, HO, full): raw signals; bets taken; skips by reason; hit rate; mean and median net return per bet; P(>= +100%); P(<= -80%); avg win / avg loss; top-5 bets' share of net P&L (or the total excluding the top 5 if the total <= 0); $1,000 equity curve, max DD, terminal equity and CAGR (HO restarted at $1,000); P(ruin) = share of 10,000 21-session block-bootstrap paths touching $500, plus P(touch $600); percentiles vs controls (a) and (b); SPY B&H. Also by year, a survivor-excluded subset (PLTR APP HOOD MSTR RKLB ASTS OKLO SMR IONQ CRWV COIN CVNA ARM SOFI HIMS AFRM UPST), the count of distinct signal weeks, a week-block bootstrap 90% CI of the mean, and the 10-day underlying return vs the matched control.
DECISION (frozen): go to live paper only if ALL hold: IS mean >= the 95th percentile of the matched control under the primary run; IS mean > 0 under stress A and E; the survivor-excluded IS mean > its matched control; HO mean > HO matched-control mean with n_HO >= 20; P(ruin) < 5%. Reading the probe's 2022 loss (-14%) as a regime warning, report the 2022 sub-period separately. A negative 2022 alone does not reject.
FEASIBILITY CHECK DONE: 435 raw signals (311 IS / 124 HO) before the per-night and concurrency caps. The 1-increment ATM vertical fits $80 on about 50%. The median price of signalled names is about $102. Most affordable spreads are 1-2 increments wide, so the maximum payoff is roughly 1-3x the debit, not the 5x of a naked call.

## Why it was picked

It was the strongest offline probe among the momentum and reversal candidates: it beat the matched leader control on the option (+14.8% vs +3.4%) and on the underlying, and survived survivor exclusion (+12.5% vs -1.5%) across about 170 distinct weeks. It is a different mechanism from P1 (trend continuation after a short-term reversal, with no catalyst), and its large HO sample (124 raw signals) allows a real out-of-sample check. It was rewritten as a budget vertical because the specified long call is unaffordable on $1,000 on 97% of signals.

## Adversarial verification

Sound: True. Final verdict: **negative**.

- Reproduced exactly. Re-running a copy (output redirected to s2v/rerun/) matches results.json and results.md to the cent: IS 11 bets, mean -0.431, $761.26; HO 4 bets, $841.71; matched percentiles 99.5 / 96.9; stress A -0.441, E -0.553.
- No look-ahead found. An independent loop re-implementation of rules 1-4 (RSpct >= 0.90, Close > SMA50 including t, a 63-day high on t-10..t-1, Close_t < min(t-5..t-1)) gives the same 1,037 pre-lockout signals with 0 mismatches. RV20, SMA50 and R126 use only data up to t. Fills use the model price on the decision close, as the spec says. Exits fall on the 10th session. The expiry is the first third Friday with DTE >= 25. Strike increments, per-leg half-spreads, $0.65 per leg per side and the floor of proceeds at 0 all match the spec. Concurrency and the governor are implemented correctly, and cash can never go negative (at most 3 x $80 open).
- The cost model makes the payoff non-monotone, so the test measures the cost model more than the signal. A 3%-of-mid half-spread on each leg's full premium means a deep-ITM vertical is worth less to exit as the stock rises. Example: a 26/27 call spread with 20 DTE at IV 0.6 nets $0.56/sh with the stock at $30, $0.18 at $40 and $0 at $50, which the engine then books as a total loss. On roughly $0.50-0.60 debits the maximum achievable return is about 0-40%. Real bets show it: CVNA +60% underlying gave -13%, NFLX +10% gave -31%, SMCI +19% gave +18%. This follows the frozen spec, but it makes the +200% asymmetric payoff the owner wants structurally impossible. It is a flaw in the spec, not a code bug.
- The 99.5th-percentile pass against the matched control is fragile and not evidence of an edge. It rests on 11 bets, all priced inside the same friction trap; the controls simply lose more because they take different (path-dependent) bets. At the signal level ($80 fixed budget, n=145) the strategy is at the 70th percentile against matched and the 14th against random. My sensitivity with a gentler combo quote (half-spread max($0.05, 5% of net spread mid), not spec) gives signal-level IS -0.139 vs a matched-pool expectation of -0.132, and HO -0.310 vs -0.240. That is no edge and still negative. The engine then takes 64 IS bets, mean -0.192, terminal $645, which is worse than the spec run.
- P(ruin) is understated. 92.6% of IS equity-curve sessions have zero return: no signals are possible before Nov 2022 because of the 252-bar rule, and the governor starves the account after June 2023. So the 21-session block bootstrap P(touch $500) = 1.8% mostly resamples flat days. The schema field ruin_probability=0 is a different metric (bet-sequence P(<$100)); the authors disclosed this.
- The governor starvation is spec-faithful, but it makes n tiny. With no trades, equity can never recover 0.85 x HWM, so the budget stays halved for good: 298 of 318 IS signals were skipped as over budget, and the HO n of 4 can never meet the n >= 20 rule. The engine-level HO percentiles (90.6 / 77.0) are meaningless at n=4.
- Split-adjusted bars inflate affordability. NFLX shows ~$26 in 2022 against a real ~$260, SMCI ~$8 against ~$80, and CVNA appears scaled by about 1/5. Most of the 11 IS bets would not have fit the $80 budget at real contract prices, and strike grids and $0.05 minimum spreads differ from what really traded.
- The naked-call diagnostic does not show signal edge either. Across all IS signals the 1.05x call averages +14.0% (median -26%) against +8.3% (median -27%) for the matched pool. HO: +10.2% vs -1.1%. Controls are positive too, which points to the survivor universe and BS drift, not to the signal. The mean is tail-driven and the medians are equal. The write-up's +9.8% on 11 bets is not significant.
- The CAGR uses the nominal 4-year IS window, including a year in which no signal can exist; the effective IS is Nov 2022 to Oct 2025. The '2022 sub-period' is only Nov-Dec 2022 (7 bets); the authors disclosed this.
- Minor: in stress E the affordability and rung choice use t+1 prices. That is a reasonable reading of a one-session delay, not a leak.
- Survivor bias is handled only partially: the survivor-excluded subset has 8 IS bets, and the matched control comes from the same 2026-chosen universe.

The NO-GO verdict holds. The implementation follows the frozen spec, I found no look-ahead, sizing or cash bugs, and the numbers reproduce exactly. The supposed positive (99.5th percentile vs the matched control) does not mean the signal works: it rests on 11 bets where everything loses, and at the signal level there is no edge under either cost model I tried.

The bigger problem is the spec itself. The cost model charges a half-spread on each leg's full premium and floors exit proceeds at $0. Together with the affordable 1-increment verticals, that caps a winning bet at about +0-40%, and a large rally can be booked as a total loss. So this design can never produce the asymmetric +200% wins the owner is after, whatever the signal does.

Any next design should use a combo-quote or hold-to-expiry exit model and a payoff that rises with the stock. It should also check affordability against unadjusted prices, because the split-adjusted history makes 2022-2023 strikes look cheaper than they really were. Option prices throughout are a Black-Scholes approximation, not real quotes.
