# P1 Gap-and-Go Catalyst Call Spread (bull regime only), from candidate S1 (catalyst lens)

Frozen before the backtest ran.

FROZEN SPEC v1 (2026-11-14). Paper research only, no orders.
HARNESS (shared by all 3 picks): Calendar = SPY trading dates only. Reindex every symbol to it and drop off-calendar bars (FFH has 27 and PSHD 36 off-calendar bars, and holiday bars caused a spurious 2026-01-19 breadth event). Data 2021-11-05..2026-11-13. IS = entries 2021-11-05..2025-10-31; HO = entries 2025-11-03..2026-11-13. RV20_u = sample stdev (ddof=1) of the last 20 daily log close-to-close returns ending u, x sqrt(252). OPTION PRICES ARE A BLACK-SCHOLES APPROXIMATION, not real quotes: European, r=4.0%, q=0, T = calendar days to expiry/365, IV_u = clip(1.25 x RV20_u, 0.15, 2.50), re-marked every night from that night's RV20 (primary). Synthetic expiries: Fridays, or the prior SPY trading day if the Friday is a holiday; 'monthly' = third Friday. Strike grid by Close: <$25 $0.50; $25-100 $1; $100-250 $2.50; >=$250 $5; 'nearest X' = round(X/inc) x inc. Costs: each leg pays half-spread h = max($0.05, 3% of leg model mid) on entry and exit (buy at mid+h, sell at max(mid-h,0)), plus $0.65 per contract per leg per side. Marks and equity use the exit side (mid-h). Fills: option entries and exits at the model price on the close of the decision day. Account: $1,000 cash at 0% interest, separate per strategy. Governor (taken from the rejected barbell): if equity < 0.85 x high-water mark, halve the target risk (the hard cap is unchanged); halt all new entries if equity < $600. Seeds: numpy default_rng(20261114).
UNIVERSE: all non-ETF symbols (exclude SPY QQQ IWM DIA TQQQ SGOV EFA IEF GLD DBC SMH QUAL VTV RSP) except SEQ, SEQUX, CBRS, GOOG (duplicate of GOOGL), FFH and PSHD (no US calendar or option chain). A symbol needs >= 250 valid bars up to and including t.
SIGNAL (close of t), all must hold: (1) Open_t/Close_{t-1} - 1 >= 0.05; (2) Volume_t >= 3.0 x mean(Volume_{t-50..t-1}); (3) (Close_t-Low_t)/(High_t-Low_t) >= 0.5, with High=Low counting as fail; (4) SPY Close_t > SMA200 of SPY Close ending t; (5) no open P1 position in that symbol. If eligible signals exceed free slots, rank by Volume_t/vol50 (descending). Live-only check, not in the backtest: IBKR earnings insight shows a report in the last 2 sessions. It is logged, and both arms are tracked in paper.
INSTRUMENT: bull call vertical, 1+ lots. Expiry = the first monthly with DTE >= 28. K1 = nearest strike to Close_t. K2 = nearest strike to 1.15 x Close_t, set to K1+inc if that is <= K1. Cost per lot = 100 x (ask(K1) - bid(K2)) + commissions. If cost > 8% of equity, lower K2 by one increment at a time, down to K1+inc. If it still does not fit, SKIP and log 'over budget'.
SIZING: lots n = floor(0.04 x equity / cost). If n=0 and cost <= 0.08 x equity, n=1. Max 4 concurrent P1 positions and total open entry cost <= 25% of equity. No adds and no averaging.
EXIT (checked each close u > t, executed at that close): (a) take profit if mark >= 2.5 x entry debit or mark >= 0.80 x width; (b) thesis stop if Close_u < Low_t; (c) time stop at the close of the 15th trading day after t; (d) expiry guard if <= 5 trading days remain to expiry. First rule hit wins. No other stop.
STRESS RUNS (all reported): A: IV mult 1.5 at entry, 1.25 thereafter. B: 1.5 everywhere. C: 1.0 everywhere (optimistic, report only). D: half-spreads doubled. E: every entry and exit delayed one session (priced at close t+1 / u+1), to test implementability.
CONTROLS: (A, primary) matched random days. For each event (symbol, calendar year), draw 1 bull-regime day in the same symbol-year with no P1 signal within +/-5 sessions. Apply identical instrument, sizing and exits; the thesis stop uses that day's Low. 1,000 draw sets; report the percentile of the strategy's mean per-bet return and of its terminal equity, each from a full $1,000 engine run per draw set. (B) non-catalyst gaps: rules 1, 3 and 4 with Volume_t < 1.5 x vol50. (C) bear regime: rules 1-3 with SPY Close_t < SMA200. (D) SPY buy-and-hold: $1,000 at Close on 2021-11-05 (and reset on 2025-10-31 for HO), price-only.
METRICS, each for IS, HO and full period: raw signals; bets taken; skips by reason (over budget, slots, concurrency cap, halt); hit rate (net P&L > 0); mean and median net return per bet on cost; P(>= +100%); P(<= -80%); avg win / avg loss; share of total net P&L from the top 5 bets (if total <= 0, report the total and the total excluding the top 5); $1,000 equity curve, max drawdown on daily marks, terminal equity and CAGR (HO restarted at $1,000); probability of ruin = share of 10,000 block-bootstrap paths (21-session blocks of the strategy's daily equity returns, resampled to the full-period length) that ever touch $500, plus P(touch $600 halt); control A percentile; difference vs controls B and C; SPY B&H return and drawdown. Also report by calendar year, a survivor-excluded subset (drop PLTR APP HOOD MSTR RKLB ASTS OKLO SMR IONQ CRWV COIN CVNA ARM SOFI HIMS AFRM UPST), and the price distribution of traded names, since the budget filter favours low-priced names.
DECISION (frozen): go to live paper only if ALL hold: IS mean per-bet return is >= the 95th percentile of control A under the primary run; IS mean > 0 under stress A and stress E; HO mean > HO control-A mean with n_HO >= 20 bets (else 'HO inconclusive', paper only); survivor-excluded IS mean > its control; P(ruin) < 5%. Otherwise reject.
FEASIBILITY CHECK DONE: 293 raw bull-regime events (228 IS / 65 HO); the narrowest ATM 1-increment spread fits $80 on 61% of them; median price of names on event days is $74.

## Why it was picked

It has the best-documented mechanism of the 8 (post-earnings drift plus the high-volume premium, though weakened in large caps), so the bet is on an event, not on a pattern. Its downside is defined (a debit spread, 4% target and 8% cap). It also has a usable holdout: 65 raw HO events, about 61% affordable on $1,000. Its edge is unstable by year, so the decision bar is control A at the 95th percentile, stress runs at IV x1.5 and a one-day delay, and a HO sign check.

## Adversarial verification

Sound: True. Final verdict: **negative**.

- None of the problems change the outcome. I re-ran backtest.py in a copy folder (s1/rerun/) and decision, control_A, ruin and the trade list came out byte-identical. A separate pandas rebuild of the signals gave the same 283 raw events (218 IS / 65 HO). A hand Black-Scholes repricing of the GM 2023-01-31 39/40 spread matched (debit 0.648, exit 0.389 on 2023-02-22, which is t+15). I found no look-ahead: vol50 is shifted, SMA200 and RV20 end at t, the thesis stop is checked at closes after entry, and exit rules fire in spec order. Sizing and cash were also correct: the 4% target uses the governor halving, the floor is 1 lot, the cap is 8% per bet and 25% total, and cash never goes negative.
- The test mostly measures the cost model, not the signal. The $1,000 account and 8% budget force 1-increment spreads ($0.50 or $1 wide, median traded name $13.40). The frozen per-leg half-spread has a $0.05 floor, so every round trip costs at least $0.20 per share plus $2.60 commissions on lots costing $25-65. In the GM example, entry is 41% above the model mid and the round trip costs about 82% of mid. The stock rose 4% and the bet still lost 43%. This structure cannot produce asymmetric wins: no take-profit ever fired, and unconstrained narrow spreads have a 0% hit rate over 275 events. REJECT is correct under the frozen spec, but the run says little about whether catalyst gaps have an edge.
- The effective IS sample is tiny. SPY's SMA200 and the 250-bar warm-up (data starts 2021-11-05) leave no eligible day before 2022-11-30, and the first event is 2023-01-26. The $600 halt then stopped entries in Aug 2023. All 15 IS bets fall in about 7 months of 2023, and 167 of 218 IS signals were never traded. The HO has 9 bets. Any percentile against control A, including the HO 93rd and 97.5th, is statistically meaningless.
- Prices are split-adjusted, so strikes, increments, the $0.05 floor and the budget check use prices that did not trade. SMCI 2023 is about $13 in the data against roughly $130 actual before the 2024 10:1 split. CVNA is about $2 in Jan 2023, which looks wrongly rescaled. Four of the 15 IS bets (2 SMCI, 2 CVNA) were on these names. In reality the SMCI trades would likely have been over budget, and the cheap CVNA trades were hit unrealistically hard by the floor. The direction of the error is unclear, and the verdict is unaffected.
- Minor deviations: vol50 uses min_periods=45 instead of a full 50 bars, though it changes no events (my full-50 rebuild matches). Control A draws per symbol-year over the full period, so 2025 draws can land in IS or HO regardless of where the matched event sits. The bootstrap includes about 1.2 years of flat pre-trading days and about 3 flat post-halt years, which dilutes P(ruin). Every variant is still far above 5% (28.6% full; 11.9% from the first entry onward), so the criterion fails either way.
- Reporting: top5_pnl_share is reported as 0, but it is undefined (results.json has null). Total P&L is negative, at -$421.8, or -$374.2 excluding the top 5. Fills at the decision-day close cannot be done live, but stress E (one-session delay) covers this and is equally negative (IS -61.2%).
- Black-Scholes with IV from nightly RV20 does not model post-earnings IV crush or real per-strike skew. This is a labelled approximation, not a bug. For a roughly vega-neutral vertical it is second order compared with the spread-cost assumption.

The backtest is correct and follows the frozen spec, so REJECT stands. The result is driven by structure, not by the signal. A $1,000 account with an 8% per-bet budget forces narrow spreads on cheap names. The spec's $0.05-per-leg minimum half-spread then takes roughly 50-80% of each bet in round-trip costs, and the payoff is capped at the spread width. That is the opposite of the asymmetric "+200%" bet the owner asked for. Untested: whether catalyst gaps carry an edge. The underlying drifted +3.4% vs +1.4% over 15 days, which is likely inflated by survivor bias, and only 15 IS bets from about 7 months of 2023 were ever traded. A redesign would need a larger notional or cheaper single long calls/combos, real option quotes from live paper logging, and unadjusted prices for strike selection.
