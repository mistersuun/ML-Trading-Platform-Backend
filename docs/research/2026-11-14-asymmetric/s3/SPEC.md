# P3 Universe-Wide Washout QQQ Call Spread, from candidate S2 index washout (reversal lens, index instrument)

Frozen before the backtest ran.

FROZEN SPEC v1 (2026-11-14). Paper research only, no orders.
HARNESS: as P1. SPY calendar only (this removes the spurious 2026-01-19 holiday 'event'). IS entries 2021-11-05..2025-10-31, HO 2025-11-03..2026-11-13. Black-Scholes APPROXIMATION: r=4%, q=0, IV_u = clip(1.25 x RV20_u of the traded ETF, 0.13, 1.00), re-marked nightly. Expiries are all Fridays, because QQQ and SPY list dailies. $1 strike grid. Index costs: half-spread per leg max($0.02, 0.5% of leg mid) each side, plus $0.65 per contract per leg per side. Fills at the model price on the decision close. $1,000 at 0%. Governor: halve target below 0.85 x HWM; halt below $600. Seed 20261114.
BREADTH UNIVERSE: every symbol in external_bars except SGOV, SEQ, SEQUX, CBRS, FFH and PSHD. RSI(2) = Wilder RSI with alpha 0.5 (ewm adjust=False on up and down close changes). breadth_t = count(RSI2 < 10) / count(valid RSI2 on t).
SIGNAL (close of t): breadth_t > 0.50 (strictly greater; the threshold is fixed and not tuned). Lockout: ignore signals within 10 sessions of the previous signal. Max 1 open P3 position; a signal while one is open is ignored. Instrument choice: QQQ, unless RV20(QQQ) > 1.5 x RV20(SPY), in which case SPY.
INSTRUMENT: bull call vertical, 1 lot. Expiry = the Friday with DTE closest to 30, ties going to the later one. K1 = nearest $1 strike to 1.02 x Close_t. K2 = K1 + W, where W is the largest of {5,4,3,2,1} dollars such that cost = 100 x (ask K1 - bid K2) + commissions <= 0.08 x equity. If W=1 does not fit, SKIP and log. Expected at $1,000: W = $2, cost about $65-80, maximum payoff about 2.5-3x the debit.
SIZING: 1 lot. Max loss = cost <= 8% of equity (governor: 4%). Bets are rare, about 4 per year, and never concurrent.
EXIT (each close u > t): (a) take profit if mark >= min(2.5 x entry debit, 0.85 x W); (b) time exit at the close of the 10th trading day after t. No stop.
STRESS RUNS: A: IV mult 1.5 at entry, 1.25 after (approximates the post-selloff IV spike above RV). B: 1.5 everywhere. C: 1.0 everywhere (report only). D: half-spreads doubled. E: one-session delay. F: always SPY instead of QQQ. G: leave-2022-out.
CONTROLS: (A, primary) matched random days. Draw the same number of QQQ (or SPY, by the same RV rule) entry days per calendar year, from days with no signal within +/-10 sessions, with an identical structure, budget rule and exits. 1,000 draw sets; report the strategy's percentile on mean per-bet return and on terminal equity. (B) systematic: the same spread entered every 3rd session, non-overlapping. (C) SPY buy-and-hold $1,000 price-only from 2021-11-05, reset on 2025-10-31. ROBUSTNESS ONLY, never used for selection: thresholds 0.40 and 0.60.
METRICS (IS, HO, full): signals; bets taken; skips; hit rate; mean and median net return per bet; P(>= +100%); P(<= -80%); avg win / avg loss; top-5 bets' share of net P&L (or the total excluding the top 5); $1,000 equity curve, max DD, terminal equity and CAGR; P(ruin) = share of 10,000 21-session block-bootstrap paths touching $500, plus P(touch $600); percentile vs control A; difference vs control B; SPY B&H; a bootstrap 90% CI over events of the mean per-bet return; QQQ's own 10-day return after events vs control.
DECISION (frozen): go to live paper only if IS mean per-bet return >= the 95th percentile of control A under the primary run AND IS mean > 0 under stress A AND under stress G (leave-2022-out) AND P(ruin) < 5%. HO: at the frozen 0.50 threshold there are 0 HO events (the maximum HO breadth was exactly 0.50, on 2025-11-17), so HO is declared INCONCLUSIVE by construction. As a non-decisive diagnostic, report the 5 HO events at threshold 0.40. Live adoption requires >= 10 forward paper events logged with real IBKR closing quotes.
FEASIBILITY CHECK DONE: 19 events, all IS (2022: 9, 2023: 4, 2024: 4, 2025: 2). A 2%-OTM $2-wide QQQ spread was modelled at $66-80 on recent events.

## Why it was picked

This is the only reversal idea whose prior survives realistic vol pricing (QQQ calls +27% vs +0.6% control in the probe). It runs on the most liquid, tightest-spread options, so the cost model is least wrong. It is market-level and rare, which makes it a genuinely different, low-capital, rarely-active third leg next to two single-name bets. It is picked with eyes open: n=19, mostly 2022, 0 holdout events at the frozen threshold, so its stress-G and forward-paper requirements are the binding tests. It was rewritten as a narrow OTM spread because a 3% premium cannot buy any QQQ call on $1,000.

## Adversarial verification

Sound: True. Final verdict: **negative**.

- No look-ahead found. Breadth, RSI2 (Wilder, alpha 0.5), RV20 and IV all use data up to and including close t, which the spec allows because fills are at the model price on the decision close. The exit-day IV uses RV at u, which is known at close u. Realistically breadth cannot be computed before the close and then traded at that same close, but stress E (one-session delay) covers this and is also negative (-56.6% mean, 5 bets).
- I independently re-implemented breadth with a loop-based RSI2. It reproduces all 20 signals exactly (19 IS, 1 HO). The 2025-11-17 breadth is 71/141 = 0.5035, so the author's flagged spec discrepancy (1 HO signal, not 0) is correct. No signal gap is exactly 10 sessions, so the ambiguity in the lockout wording ('>= 10' vs '> 10') changes nothing.
- I independently re-priced bet 1 (2022-01-19 QQQ 374/375, IV 0.2876, 30 DTE). The debit is 0.4853, the cost is $49.83 and the exit proceeds are $30.91, all matching. The W=2 cost was $87.86, which is over the $80 budget, so W=1 is correct.
- Re-running the unmodified script, with only the output directory changed, gave a results.json identical to the original apart from runtime. The results are deterministic under seed 20261114.
- The headline rests on 6 bets, all in 2022 and highly correlated, because of the spec's governor. After the 6th loss, equity is $806.41, below 0.85 x HWM, so the budget falls to 4% ($32). That is below the roughly $50 cost of the cheapest $1-wide spread, so all 13 later IS signals are skipped permanently. This is a spec design flaw, not a code bug. The path-free diagnostic (all 19 IS signals, fixed $80 budget) is still negative: mean -27.5%, 90% CI [-47%, -6%], 31st percentile vs matched days. So the negative verdict does not depend on the lockout.
- Structural flaw against the owner's asymmetric goal: at $1,000 with 2022-2025 IV, the budget forces W=$1, with a debit of about $0.49 to $0.58. The take-profit is min(2.5 x debit, 0.85 x W) = $0.85, so the best possible net return is about +70% to +75% after the bid-side exit and commissions. P(>= +100%) is close to 0 by construction for W=1 bets. The 'occasional large win' this strategy is meant to produce cannot happen at this account size.
- The claimed top5_pnl_share of 0.747 is a meaningless ratio of two negative numbers, and the script itself reports n/a. The useful figure is the total excluding the top 5, -$48.93. For stress G, the +$3.99 total depends entirely on 4 winners: the total excluding the top 5 is -$171.45 and the median is -13.5%. G 'passing' the mean > 0 gate is noise, as the author notes.
- ruin_probability = 0 comes from a bootstrap over only 6 bets, so it is uninformative. The spec's 21-session block bootstrap gives P(touch $500) = 0.000 and P(touch $600) = 0.003. That figure reflects the governor halting trading, not the strategy being safe. With no governor, the expected value is negative and a 100-bet bootstrap gives ruin with probability 1.0.
- Minor: in stress E the 10-day time exit is counted from the delayed entry (ne + 10), not from t. Exits use the model bid on the long leg and the ask on the short leg, plus 0.5% half-spreads, while take-profit fires on the mid. That is slightly harsh on deep-ITM exits but consistent with the spec. Flat Black-Scholes vol with no skew is an approximation. Stress runs A to D bracket the IV and cost uncertainty, and all are negative.
- Survivorship affects only the breadth universe, whose valid count rises from 131 to 141 symbols as later listings enter. The traded instruments are QQQ and SPY, and control A uses the same universe and the same per-year quotas (6 in 2022), from days with no breadth > 0.50 within +/-10 sessions. That is a fair, if tiny, control. The strategy's 0.4th percentile vs control A is over n = 6 and shows these washout days did worse than random 2022 days. QQQ's 10-day return after them was -2.6% vs +1.4% for control days.

I checked the backtest line by line, re-ran it, and independently re-implemented breadth, the signals and one bet's pricing. Everything matches. The NO-GO decision is sound and holds without the drawdown governor: at the signal level, washout entries do no better than random days. The deeper problem for the owner's goal is that at $1,000 the 8% budget forces $1-wide spreads, which cannot pay +100% or more. Any revised asymmetric spec would need a larger budget per bet, wider or cheaper structures, or longer holds, and a governor that cannot lock itself out permanently.
