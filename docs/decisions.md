# Decisions (ADR log)

Each decision lists when to revisit it. The sources are `docs/research/robustness-roadmap.md` and `docs/research/portfolio-recommendations.md`.

## D1 — The platform is a research and allocation cockpit, not an autonomous trader (2026-10-04)
- **Decision:** The platform is a research screen with alerts. Allocation changes are produced as rebalance *proposals*, and the owner places them manually in IBKR. Unattended orders are allowed only in Alpaca **paper**, and only after the Phase 1 chokepoint and the Phase 2 multiple-testing gate exist.
- **Revisit:** after 18–24 months of paper results that pass the graduation gates.

## D2 — Paper only, no live mode
- **Decision:** `paper=True` stays hard-coded in `paper_trader.py` and is enforced by a test. Roadmap WS1.6 "live mode" is dropped.
- **Revisit:** only as a separate, explicit owner decision after D1's gates pass.

## D3 — What can be traded
- **Decision:** In paper, only US-listed ETFs and US large-cap stocks can be traded, long-only. There are no shorts, no margin and no leveraged or inverse ETFs. A SELL signal means "exit the long", and its quantity is capped at the quantity held. Crypto, forex and futures are research-only. Pairs trading is a diagnostic only. Drop GC/SI and CL/NG from the default pairs scan.
- **Revisit:** if a signal family passes the D6 gates and needs a different instrument.

## D4 — Portfolio design
- **Decision:** The core is about 90% of capital: 32% US, 18% developed ex-US, 5% EM, 13% intermediate Treasuries, 7% long Treasuries, 7% TIPS, 8% T-bills, 5% gold and 0–5% commodities. It is rebalanced by bands, with no stops. A trend sleeve of about 10% uses a Faber 8/10/12-month moving average and T-bills when off. The platform's own signals get 0% of real capital and at most 5% later, and only if they pass the gates.
- **Caveat:** none of this has been validated on a long history yet. A long-history proxy backtest (1990+, ideally 1972+) is required before the core's drawdown expectations are relied on.
- **Revisit:** after the long-history backtest, and once a year after that.

## D5 — Risk defaults for the signal sleeve (to be implemented in Phase 1; Phase 0 only adds interlocks)
- 0.5% risk per trade, with the stop at 2×ATR(20) from the fill.
- At most 10% of the sleeve in any one symbol, and at most 4% total risk to stops ("heat").
- Gross exposure at most 100%, with no leverage.
- At most 8 positions, and at most 2 per cluster.
- At most 5 new orders a day.
- Daily and weekly entry stops at -1.5% and -3%.
- A drawdown ladder at -5%, -8% and -10%, with a persisted, fail-closed halt.
- Each order is capped at min(10% of the sleeve, $1000) during plumbing tests.
- Sleeve equity is a configured dollar amount, never taken from the broker's account equity.

## D6 — Signal graduation gates
- **Decision:** Statistical evidence must come from a **pre-registered backtest** on a fixed calendar hold-out. It must pass a BH/Holm correction across every hypothesis tested and have a Deflated Sharpe p below 0.05. Paper trading is an implementation check only: divergence from the backtest within tolerance, and zero breaches. Scaling up requires PSR(SR>0) ≥ 0.95 on paper returns.

## D7 — Toolchain (Phase 0, WS0.1)
- **Decision:** Python 3.11 (the range 3.11–3.12 is allowed), with uv, `pyproject.toml` and a committed `uv.lock`. `requirements.txt` is generated with `uv export`. pandas is pinned to `~=2.3`, because 3.x changes how the patterns compute.
- **Removed from the dependencies:** pandas-ta, matplotlib, seaborn and rich, which nothing imports; and vectorbt and backtesting, which were only used by optional, guarded imports in `backtester.py` whose results are never used. xgboost is **kept**, because removing it would change ML results before a baseline exists.
- **Dev tools:** pytest, pytest-socket, hypothesis, httpx and ruff.
- **Revisit:** move to pandas 3 as a separate migration, run with `-W error::FutureWarning`.

## D8 — Tests before fixes
- **Decision:** Every known bug is pinned by a test marked `xfail(strict=True, raises=AssertionError)` and written against today's public interface. A fix must flip its test to passing and remove the marker in the same commit.
- **Baseline:** a frozen data snapshot and a metrics report act as the baseline. Every Phase 2 change attaches a before/after diff of that report.


## D9 — SIG-1 fixed early (WS0.4)
The check_recent_signal fix (latest non-zero signal wins) landed in Phase 0 rather than WS1.5 because order direction depends on it; it also changes alert direction. ML order confidence is taken from the bar of that signal. Risk-free-rate and uninvested-cash yield convention for Sharpe is still open (decide in WS2.2 before BT-5 is flipped).


## D10 — Phase 1 as built (execution, risk state, alerts, LLM, allocation)
- **No live mode.** `TRADING_MODE` is `off` or `paper`; the Alpaca client is always `paper=True` (tested). Long-only, whole shares, equities/ETFs only (`instruments.py` is the registry). Orders need paper mode, `PAPER_TRADE_ENABLED`, an initialised state DB, and a `validation_status` in `config.ORDER_ELIGIBLE_STATUSES`. Nothing reaches that in Phase 1, so scans are alert-only and paper decisions are `rejected: not_validated`.
- **One chokepoint.** `execution.submit_intent` runs risk gate, mode, intent/registry/validation checks, broker reconciliation, a unique-key signal ledger (one intent per symbol/bar), ATR sizing with the D5 caps, bracket BUYs, and deterministic `client_order_id`s. Timeouts are resolved by lookup, never by resubmitting. `paper_trader.py` is a shim with no raw order path.
- **Persisted risk state** in SQLite (`state/`, WAL, migrations 001-002): halt, peak, day/week baselines, orders per day, account baseline. Fail-closed: `update_equity()` rejects NaN/inf/<=0, and `check()` blocks (`equity_not_updated`) until a reading exists. Sleeve equity = `SIGNAL_SLEEVE_EQUITY + (paper account equity - baseline)`, where the baseline (account equity minus open P&L) is captured at the first trading session, so realized losses persist after a stop fills. This is consistent with D5 only because the paper account is dedicated to the sleeve (deposits, withdrawals or manual trades there distort it; reset by `risk init` on a fresh DB). The latest equity reading is held in memory, so every run refreshes it (`execution.refresh_sleeve_equity`) before `check()`. A depleted sleeve (<= 0) halts.
- **CLI.** `main.py` has subcommands (`scan`, `risk init|status|halt|resume|reconcile`, `check-llm`, `rebalance`, `backup`); the old flat flags alias `scan`. Each run reconciles first (paper mode), records a `runs` row, and sends alerts (`signal`, `order_decision`, `halt`, `reconcile_mismatch`, `briefing_failed`) after the Decision, with the mode, deduplicated via `alerts_sent` (order-decision keys are built from the intent, `signal_key|status`, because decisions made before the ledger key exists carry no `client_order_id`). Pairs are never executed (they need a short leg). GC/SI and CL/NG are dropped from the default pairs scan.
- **Alerts and LLM hardened** (WS1.7, WS1.8): Telegram HTML escaping and limits, retries, log redaction; the briefing uses the Anthropic SDK with structured output, never imports the order path, and a failure sends one `briefing_failed` alert and the run continues.
- **Allocation** (D1/D4) is advisory: `main.py rebalance` prints a proposal from latest prices and 13 monthly closes; the owner places trades.
- **Deviations from the roadmap, recorded.** (1) WS1.4 says a daily loss halts and a new UTC day does not clear it; D5 is binding, so the daily (-1.5%) and weekly (-3%) limits are entry stops that roll over with the UTC day / ISO week, not persisted halts. Only the -10% drawdown halt persists. That acceptance line is superseded. (2) `CANCEL_ON_HALT` is implemented: on a halt or kill switch at session open or during dispatch, unfilled parent BUY entries placed by this platform are cancelled and an alert is sent; protective stop/take-profit legs are never cancelled. (3) Bracket entries are `gtc` (legs inherit the parent's TIF, so `day` would drop the stop on day 2) and `reconcile` raises `missing_protective_stop` for any platform-bracketed long with no live stop or in-flight exit; a SELL exit first cancels that position's legs, then sells. This rests on Alpaca's bracket rules, which could not be re-verified against the official docs from the build environment: the README checklist makes it an owner check. (4) Definitive broker refusals (4xx other than 408/429, order not found) mark the signal `rejected` and do not block the sleeve; timeouts, 5xx and transport errors stay `unknown` and block until `risk reconcile --accept`. (5) Pending entries count toward the gross and heat caps. (6) Relative `STATE_DB_PATH` / `KILL_SWITCH_FILE` resolve against the repo, not the cwd.
- **Exit protocol and guards (review round 2).** A SELL exit of a bracketed long is cancel -> confirm -> sell -> re-protect. Only legs attributable to this platform (id in the `orders` table, nested under one of our parents, or an un-nested leg matching a bracket's symbol + qty + price) are cancelled; a 404/422 on a cancel means already gone (async cancel, sibling auto-cancelled). Open orders are then polled (bounded by `EXIT_CANCEL_WAIT_SECONDS`) until the legs are terminal, because a `pending_cancel` leg still reserves its shares. If the SELL then fails, a standalone GTC stop (the cancelled stop's price, else the recorded stop, else ATR-based) is re-placed for the held qty and the ledger row becomes `rejected_retryable` (the next run retries; never `rejected`); if the stop cannot be re-placed either, the sleeve halts with an `UNPROTECTED <sym>` halt event that `main.py` alerts on. A reduce-only SELL runs through any reconcile mismatch except `broker_error` and `short_position` (a missing stop must not block the exit that fixes it); acceptance (`risk reconcile --accept`) counts only from the latest snapshot and expires per symbol when a new platform bracket is placed on it; a stop smaller than the position is `insufficient_protective_stop`. An unreadable broker book (`positions is None`) refuses the session and never seeds the baseline.
- **Equity-jump guard.** Seeding the account baseline raises an alert event. A rise of the sleeve equity of more than `EQUITY_JUMP_HALT_PCT` (10%) between consecutive readings with no new `orders` row in between (so no platform fill can explain it: a deposit or manual trade in the account, D10) halts; a fall of that size already trips the drawdown halt. The operator resolves it with `risk resume --confirm` or `risk rebaseline --confirm` (clears the baseline, last reading and peak; re-seeded, with an alert, at the next session). `risk init` prints the dedicated-paper-account assumption.
- **Before anything becomes order-eligible (Phase 2):** realized P&L comes from the account-equity baseline above, not from a fills ledger; reconcile it against Alpaca account activities before trusting the daily/weekly stops on real signals.
- **Still open:** the owner's one manual verification on a real Alpaca paper account (see README checklist); take-profit is a fixed 3 x ATR constant in `execution.py`; entry/stop prices use the last close, to be reconciled with the Phase 2 backtest engine.


## D11 — Phase 2 as built (correct results: engine, data, validation, ML, pairs, API)
- **Engine convention (`engine.py`, `metrics.py`).** Signals are computed at bar t's close and filled at the **next bar's open** plus slippage (`EXECUTION_MODE = "next_open"`; `"close"` is an explicit opt-in used only by characterization tests). Stops and targets are checked against each bar's high/low, a gap through a level fills at the open, and the stop wins a same-bar tie. The equity curve is marked to market every bar. **Sharpe uses rf = 0 on daily mark-to-market returns of the ACTUAL position size** (closes the Sharpe question left open in D9); Sortino, Calmar, CAGR and PSR follow the same returns. `Trade.pnl_pct` is net of costs as a fraction of position notional. `backtester.classic_backtest` / `BacktestResult` stay as a compatibility wrapper (API, CLI, Streamlit); `BacktestResult.is_valid` is a **display heuristic only** and never gates an order.
- **Hold-out (D6 made concrete).** The hold-out is every bar on or after the fixed calendar date `config.HOLDOUT_START` (never "the last 20%", so it does not move when data is appended). It is never used for selection, parameter search, sensitivity or model fitting. **Rule: a hold-out is read once per strategy version.** Looking at the hold-out result and then changing the strategy, its parameters or the candidate set makes it a validation set; the version must be re-registered and the hold-out date moved forward (a new decision entry) before it counts again. `validation.holdout_eval` starts flat at the hold-out date; a flat hold-out (zero trades) returns 0 and passes the `>= 0` gate, which is what the spec says and is a known weakness (it is blocked in practice by the OOS-trade and PSR gates).
- **Multiple testing.** Every candidate (symbol x pattern x stop/target) tested in a run enters ONE Benjamini-Hochberg correction at `FDR_ALPHA` on the random-entry-null p-values (`NULL_DRAWS` draws, seeded from `config.SEED`). The Deflated Sharpe is computed and reported but advisory; Phase 4 gates on it (`deflated_validated` is not produced yet). `oos_validated` requires ALL of: BH-significant, at least `MIN_TRADES_OOS` OOS trades, OOS PSR above `OOS_PSR_MIN`, hold-out return >= 0, positive return at `COST_STRESS_MULT` x costs. The one-bar delay stress is recorded, not gated.
- **Data layer (`data/`).** Alpha Vantage is removed. Sources: Alpaca (equities, ETFs, crypto; `adjustment=all`, `ALPACA_FEED`, `end` clamped 16 minutes back) and yfinance (`auto_adjust=True`). A source is pinned to a symbol after its first valid fetch and never silently replaced. Bars are validated (index, OHLC consistency, coverage >= `DATA_COVERAGE_MIN`, staleness, calendar sessions via `exchange-calendars`); in-progress sessions are dropped; a large daily move (`DATA_MAX_ABS_DAILY_RETURN`) is flagged, not dropped. `fetch_ohlcv` returns an empty frame on failure; `fetch_bars` raises `DataQualityError` / `DataUnavailableError`. Pins DATA-2 and DATA-3 originally asserted Alpha Vantage parsing; they were retargeted to the remaining adapters under D12.
- **ML (`ml/`, `ml_patterns.py`, `features.py`).** Labels are NaN where the horizon is unresolved; CV is a purged, embargoed time split (`ML_HORIZON_BARS`, `ML_EMBARGO_BARS`); no scaler; probabilities are walk-forward and out-of-sample only after `oos_start` with periodic retraining. `scan_ml` uses `ml_scan_candidate`; a signal is reported only when OOS AUC exceeds the baseline AUC (0.5 plus the one-sided 95% no-skill margin). ML validation status is at best `ml_oos_candidate` (superseded by D12: it is NOT in `ORDER_ELIGIBLE_STATUSES`, so an ML intent can never reach a broker): the candidate's own gates pass (AUC above baseline, >= `MIN_TRADES_OOS` OOS trades, PSR strictly above `OOS_PSR_MIN`) AND Benjamini-Hochberg across the ML candidates of that run (p = 1 - OOS PSR) rejects. ML stays alert-only until, in Phase 3, it passes the same random-entry null, 2x-cost and fixed hold-out gates inside the technical BH family; the OOS block still starts at `ML_TRAIN_TEST_SPLIT` of the available history, not at `HOLDOUT_START`.
- **Pairs (`pairs_trading.py`).** The spread and z-score come from a causal rolling OLS (row t is fitted on bars t-w..t-1); Engle-Granger is run in both orderings (max p) and BH-corrected across all pairs in a scan; the half-life is `None` unless 0 < phi < 1; entries need |z| strictly between entry and stop; P&L is return-on-gross, never divided by the spread level. **Pairs remain alert-only (D3):** they need a short leg and never produce an order intent. Pair backtests are in-sample; alerts and the report say so.
- **Technical scan (`main.scan_technical`).** One `validation.evaluate_candidates` call over the whole run on `RESEARCH_LOOKBACK_DAYS` of history (so BH spans every candidate), then each intent carries that candidate's `validation_status`. Alerts and the HTML report show OUT-OF-SAMPLE metrics, the BH-adjusted p-value, the hold-out return and the status, never the in-sample numbers. A signal is reported if it is validated, or OOS-positive with enough OOS trades (labelled `unvalidated`), or, as a **legacy informational tier to be retired in Phase 3**, if the old in-sample `is_valid` heuristic passes (labelled `unvalidated`, with OOS fields `n/a`). The legacy tier exists only so existing alert-only behaviour and its pins keep working; it can never reach a broker.
- **What is now order-eligible.** Only an intent whose `validation_status` is in `config.ORDER_ELIGIBLE_STATUSES` (`oos_validated`, `deflated_validated`) and that passes every Phase 1 gate (D2, D3, D5, D10): US equities and ETFs only, long-only, paper only. In practice a technical candidate must survive the full gate set above, validated as the EXECUTABLE variant (D12); ML candidates are not order-eligible. Expect very few, possibly none, on real data: that is the intended outcome of correcting for multiple testing. If validation itself errors, the scan logs an error and nothing from it is order-eligible.
- **API (WS2.7).** Every route returns strict JSON (`api.serialize.SafeJSONResponse`, non-finite -> `null`); every failure is non-2xx with `{error: {code, message, details}}` (`api.errors`): validation 422, no data or unknown symbol 404, data-quality failure 502, unexpected 500 with a request id and no internals. Ratios are fractions (`total_return`, `max_drawdown`, `win_rate`); legacy `*_pct` / formatted-string keys (`total_return_pct` in scans, `metrics_display` for backtests) are kept for one release and then removed. Request bounds: `period_days` 60..3650, `n_splits` 2..12, symbols match a ticker pattern, pattern names are in `PATTERN_REGISTRY`, pair legs must differ. Symbols are format-validated, not registry-validated: many research symbols are legitimately outside `instruments`, and an unknown ticker surfaces as 404 `no_data` from the data layer. Stress and sensitivity routes fetch at least `RESEARCH_LOOKBACK_DAYS`; scans return per-symbol/pattern failures in `failed[]` and log them.
- **Still open / deviations.** The five unsatisfiable pins (BT-1b, BT-5, PR-4, DATA-2, DATA-3) were rewritten under D12. Pairs use a full-sample half-life for the time stop and fill at the signal close with daily-rebalanced constant-beta legs (Phase 4: fixed-share legs, next-open fills, walk-forward half-life, plus the dollar-P&L additive-shift test); alerts stay labelled in-sample until then. `dashboard.py` (Streamlit) got minimal None / InsufficientData guards (`dashboard_fmt.py`) rather than a full migration. Open owner action: the real-data three-run baseline (docs/baseline/README.md).


## D12 — Review round 2 (executable variant, ML gate, null resolution, hold-out read-once, pins)
- **ML is not order-eligible.** `main.scan_ml` gives a candidate that clears its own gates + the ML-only BH the status `ml_oos_candidate` (`signals.ML_CANDIDATE`), which is deliberately outside `config.ORDER_ELIGIBLE_STATUSES`. The ML PSR gate is now strictly `> OOS_PSR_MIN`. Tests: gates pass but BH fails -> unvalidated; PSR 0.94 / 0.95 -> not validated; an ML intent is never in an eligible status. Raising ML to `oos_validated` is a Phase 3 decision (needs null + 2x cost + fixed hold-out, one BH family).
- **Validate what is executed.** `engine.run_backtest` has `allow_short=False` (a -1 signal only closes a long) and ATR exits (`atr_stop_mult` / `atr_target_mult`: levels anchored on the signal bar's CLOSE with ATR(`ATR_PERIOD`) at the signal bar, as `execution` submits its bracket). `validation.evaluate_candidates(executable_variant=True)` (what `scan_technical` uses) validates only that variant: long-only, stop `ATR_STOP_MULT` x ATR, target `TAKE_PROFIT_ATR_MULT` x ATR, so a short-side or percentage-exit edge cannot validate a BUY. The random-entry null replicates the ATR exits. Remaining difference from live: position sizing (the backtest uses `MAX_POSITION_SIZE_PCT`; live sizes by ATR risk with the D5 caps) and the live fill is a market order at the open, not the backtest's open + slippage.
- **Null resolution matches the correction.** The Monte Carlo p-value cannot go below 1/(draws+1), but BH needs p <= alpha/m for the best of m candidates (6.4e-5 for 780). `validation.adaptive_null` re-draws a candidate that is still a BH contender (p <= alpha) with 10x more draws until 1/(draws+1) < alpha/m or `MAX_NULL_DRAWS` (200k); non-contenders cost nothing extra. Slow test: 39 symbols x 20 patterns, one planted edge, BH rejects exactly that candidate.
- **Hold-out is read once per strategy version.** `state/holdout.py` (separate `holdout_reads.sqlite` next to the state DB, no state-DB migration) stores the FIRST hold-out evaluation per (strategy version, symbol|candidate key); later scans gate on the stored result (`holdout_frozen`, `holdout_asof` on the result). Strategy version = hash of the pattern source, exits/sides, cost model and hold-out date. Changing any of them is a new version and a new read (by D11 the owner must also move `HOLDOUT_START` forward when iterating on a viewed hold-out). Display routes (`/backtest/run`, `/ml/predict`) label the hold-out share (`holdout` block, per-trade `in_holdout`) and accept `include_holdout=false`; `full_stress_test` regimes and Monte Carlo use pre-hold-out bars only.
- **Pins rewritten (not weakened).** BT-1b: overnight-gap canary `sig[t] = sign(open[t+1] - close[t])`, which must be profitable with fills at the signal close and not with next-open fills (the old canary peeked at close[t+1] while open[t+1] ~ close[t], so no engine could remove it). BT-5: path-consistent random walk (open == previous close, sub-bar high/low), stop/target off, 100 seeds, |mean Sharpe| < 0.25 (the old wick model biased stop/target touches). PR-4: the contract (200, strict JSON, integer count) on the original fixture, which is cointegrated at p = 0.0508 and is rightly rejected, plus PR-4b: a clearly cointegrated pair is returned. DATA-2 / DATA-3: the same invariants on the yfinance / Alpaca fakes (the Alpha Vantage parser they targeted is deleted).
- **Waived: backtesting.py cross-check (WS2.2 acceptance).** D7 removed the dependency; closed-form golden fixtures (tests/test_engine_golden.py) are the engine's reference instead. No dev-only `backtesting` dependency is added.
- **Annualisation uses the instrument registry** (`instruments.bars_per_year`: 365 crypto, 260 fx, 252 otherwise) in `classic_backtest`, `evaluate_candidates`, hold-out and Monte Carlo.
- **Data pins are per (symbol, window class)**: the research window (>= `RESEARCH_LOOKBACK_DAYS`) pins on its own, so a short-history vendor pinned by the 730-day fetch cannot block the 8-year research fetch.
- **Legacy `walk_forward_validate` folds** now have contiguous, non-overlapping test windows (display-only; `fold_scheme` in the API response). ML dead-volume columns are decided on rows before `oos_start`; `oos_start` is still a fraction of the history (anchoring it to a date would put the ML OOS block on the hold-out).
- **Phase 4:** pairs engine accounting (fixed-share legs, next-open fills, walk-forward half-life, additive-shift dollar-P&L test).

## D13 — Phase 3 as built (services, contracts, data, nightly job, models, settings)
- **Layers.** `services/` holds all use cases (scan, backtest, pairs, ml, stress, market, report, data_status, session) behind a `DataProvider` protocol; `main.py` is argparse plus services and `routes/*.py` are thin adapters (`Depends(get_provider)` so tests inject a fixture provider). An import-graph test enforces the direction (adapters never import domain modules; services never import adapters) and a parity test pins that the CLI and `POST /api/patterns/scan` produce the same candidates, differing only by the OOS gate. The legacy in-sample-only "candidate" tier is retired: reported candidates are validated or OOS-positive.
- **Typed contracts.** `response_model` only on the 13 endpoints the frontend calls; `api.errors.reply()` validates every payload and returns 500 `contract_violation` on a real mismatch (non-finite floats become null). `openapi.json` is committed, checked by `tests/test_openapi_snapshot.py` and `scripts/export_openapi.py --check`; the frontend generates `schema.d.ts` from it (`npm run api:check` in CI).
- **Bar store.** Parquet files under `data/bars/` with atomic writes, incremental refresh (re-adjustment detected by overlap comparison, then full refetch), per-(symbol, window) source pins in `bar_sources`, stale-serve when the pinned vendor fails, `data-status` CLI and `GET /api/data/status`. Network resilience is `tenacity`-style retries with backoff and Retry-After only.
- **Heavy work.** A nightly CLI run (`main.py run-nightly`, `schedule`, `heartbeat`; 17:30 America/New_York, `fcntl` lock, `runs` row, state backup, deduplicated failure alert, heartbeat alert kind) writes atomic result files under `results/<kind>/`, served read-only by `GET /api/results/{kind}/latest` with a stale flag (> 30 h). Interactive heavy routes (patterns scan, ml predict, stress full/sensitivity, pairs scan) use `api.concurrency.heavy_endpoint(1)` and return 429 `busy` when their slot is taken (one slot per endpoint; routes stay plain `def`).
- **Models.** Versioned joblib bundles with metadata under `models/<symbol>/<timestamp>/`; loads refuse a major-version or feature-schema mismatch; no pickle. Display path reuses the latest valid model and retrains after `ML_RETRAIN_DAYS`; walk-forward never touches the store. Wiring `services.ml.retrain` into the nightly run is still open.
- **Settings.** `settings.py` (pydantic-settings) covers only `API_TOKEN` (bearer on `/api/*` except health), `CORS_ORIGINS` (default `http://localhost:5173`) and `API_BIND_HOST`, plus fail-fast validation of the existing risk config. README has the runbook.
- **Trimmed on purpose (owner-approved reviewer advice).** No SQLite job table or thread-pool job framework; no token bucket or circuit breaker; no `response_model` on unused endpoints; `config.py` was not ported to pydantic-settings; routes stay `def`. Revisit only if a measured need appears.
- **Open.** Loose `dict` models (`metrics`, `spread_data`, stress report) keep those schemas weak; `joblib` is a transitive dependency only; `API_BIND_HOST` is informational (uvicorn `--host` decides).
